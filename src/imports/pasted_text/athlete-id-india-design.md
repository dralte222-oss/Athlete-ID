You are an award-winning Senior UI/UX Designer and Front-End Developer with over 10 years of experience designing products for companies like Nike, Strava, Garmin, Apple, and LinkedIn. Your task is to design and generate a complete, production-ready website for my startup called Athlete ID India.

The website must be modern, premium, minimalistic, responsive, and user-centric. The final output should include clean HTML5, CSS3, and vanilla JavaScript (no frameworks). Use semantic HTML, modular CSS, smooth animations, and optimized performance.

Brand Information

Brand Name: Athlete ID India

Tagline: One Athlete. One Identity. Unlimited Opportunities.

About the Startup

Athlete ID India is a digital platform that provides every athlete with a verified digital identity. It allows athletes to create a lifelong sports profile containing verified achievements, rankings, certificates, match history, statistics, performance data, videos, and career opportunities.

The platform connects athletes with coaches, academies, scouts, universities, recruiters, sponsors, clubs, and sports organizations.

The overall experience should communicate trust, innovation, professionalism, and the future of sports technology.
